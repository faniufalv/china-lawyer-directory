## Q1: For Overseas Chinese: How to apply for disability assessment after a traffic accident?

**A**: Traffic accident disability assessment process: 1) Apply to qualified judicial appraisal institution after treatment completion; 2) Submit accident report, medical records, imaging materials; 3) Institution issues assessment within 30 working days; 4) Dissatisfied parties can request re-assessment.

**法律依据**: 《民法典》侵权责任编

**领域**: 交通事故 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Criminal Defense] For Expatriates: How is property divided in cross-border divorce?

**A**: Cross-border divorce property division involves multiple legal systems: 1) Determine jurisdiction court first; 2) Chinese property follows Chinese law, overseas property may follow local law; 3) Different countries have different standards for marital property; 4) Prenuptial agreements recommended.

**法律依据**: 《民事诉讼法》涉外编

**领域**: 婚姻家事 | **语言**: English
